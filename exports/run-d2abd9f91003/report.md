## Summary

The 'Finance' niche on YouTube reveals a diverse ecosystem of 115 channels, ranging from multi-million subscriber educators to micro-creators. Content strategies are highly varied, including localized advice for specific regions (India, Philippines, Nigeria, the UK), sensationalist stock-market commentary, reality-style 'fix your finance' interviews, and actionable 'hack' videos. A key finding is the performance of mid-sized and small channels, which frequently achieve viral outlier hits, often driven by algorithm-friendly practical tips or provocative topics, achieving views far exceeding their typical subscriber base and engagement ratios. The 'Investing & Market Analysis' branch is dominated by high-volume Indian creators and urgent US stock-pickers, while 'Personal Finance & Wealth Building' is characterized by niche communities like cash-envelope budgeting and a strong appetite for quick money-saving hacks. Overall, the niche is not defined by rigid sub-clusters but by overlapping audiences, common keyword use, and a clear division between measured, data-driven educational content and sensationalist, high-urgency financial promotion. The inability to graph-identify formal sub-nodes suggests these audiences frequently cross between investing, saving, and personal finance content.
